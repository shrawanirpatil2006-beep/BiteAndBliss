Our Story

A Little Bakery with a Big Heart

Welcome to Bite & Bliss — a cozy neighbourhood bakery tucked away on Maple Street, where every recipe is a love letter to classic baking. Founded by Shrawani Patil, our bakery started as a small home kitchen dream and grew into a beloved community spot.

We source the finest local ingredients — farm-fresh eggs, organic flour, and real dairy butter — to craft treats that taste just like grandma used to make, only a little more Instagram-worthy. 😊
